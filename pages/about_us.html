Our Story & Ethos

Founded on the conviction that football is more than 90 minutes of competition, We Are Eleven unites players, organizers, and supporters across Kolkata and beyond to celebrate the beautiful game.

What began as casual weekend turf matches has evolved into an official grassroots football league ecosystem with structured annual tournaments, competitive player auctions, certified refereeing, and an active digital community.

"We are more than just eleven players on the pitch; we are a family united by passion, resilience, and sportsmanship. Together, we play, we conquer, We Are Eleven."
