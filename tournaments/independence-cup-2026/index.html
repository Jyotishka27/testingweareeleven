Tournament Format & Progression Pathway

From the live auction floor to the championship podium.

1. Player Auction Phase 1

6 Captains bid from a ₹3.50L budget across Elite, Prime, Core, and Developing tiers, drafting 42 athletes into 8-man squads.

2. Group Stage (12) Phase 2

Double round-robin league play in Groups A & B (4 games each). Casual United topped Group A and Japan FC topped Group B.

3. Cross Semifinals Phase 3

Both cross-group semifinals finished 0-0 and went to sudden-death penalty shootouts. Japan FC (2-0) and 8zilla (2-1) advanced.

4. Grand Final Champion

Japan FC defeated 8zilla 2-0 with an Arkapal masterclass to hoist the Independence Cup 2026 trophy undefeated.

Official Tournament Rules & Match Regulations

Standard operating guidelines enforced across Independence Cup 2026 fixtures.

Official WAE Manual

🏟️ Field, Equipment & Team Structure

  • Pitch: 70 × 110 ft synthetic turf arena at GODs Turf with official marked sidelines.
  • Football: Standard FIFA-approved Size 5 match ball.
  • Squad Size: 8 players registered per franchise (1 Captain + 7 Auction picks).
  • On-Pitch Format: 6 players active on field at once (1 Goalkeeper + 5 Outfield players).
  • Substitutions: Rolling substitutions permitted during dead-ball stoppages.

⏱️ Timing, Points & Tie-Breakers

  • Match Duration: 16 minutes per fixture (8-minute halves with 2-minute interval).
  • Points System: Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point, Loss = 0 points.
  • Group Ranking Criteria: Points → Goal Difference (GD) → Goals For (GF) → Head-to-Head.
  • Knockout Ties: Any draw at the end of regulation time proceeds immediately to a sudden-death penalty shootout.

🟨 Discipline & Referee Authority

  • Yellow Cards: Serves as an official caution; logged on tournament match sheets.
  • Red Cards: Automatic dismissal from current match plus a mandatory 1-match suspension.
  • Referee Decisiveness: The referee's on-pitch decisions regarding fouls, handballs, and goals are strictly final.

⚠️ Safety, Footwear & Medical Advisory

  • Footwear: Turf shoes, multi-ground studs, or athletic sports joggers only. No metal studs allowed.
  • Safety: No jewelry or sharp accessories during active play.
  • Hydration & First-Aid: Teams must maintain individual water bottles and basic personal medical kits.
